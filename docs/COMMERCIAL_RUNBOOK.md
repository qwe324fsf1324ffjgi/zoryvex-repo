# Commercial Runbook — v4.5 Optimization & Cost Discipline Addendum (full text)

Recorded verbatim from the founder's preserved manual, linked from [[docs/LOOP_RUNBOOK]]. This addendum preserves every v4.4 requirement and does not change the fixed product, three launch tracks, candidate-free rule, prices, first-25 Founding Employer rule, no-annual-plan rule, Abbas Solution disclosure, provider boundary, architecture, security controls, legal gates, safe-shell fallback, or pass phrases.

## 1. Commercial objective

Maximize durable founder cash flow, retained employer revenue, and long-term enterprise value — never promise wealth or optimize vanity user counts. "Best" means most useful, trustworthy, fast, accessible, reliable, maintainable, and cost-efficient while passing the real customer gate — not most features. "High demand" and "low competition" are hypotheses, never published without current recorded evidence. No fabricated traction, urgency, scarcity, reviews, employers, candidates, or outcomes.

## 2. One primary launch offer

`proof_hiring_kit_v1` is the primary launch revenue path: free role diagnostic → truthful proof-gap preview → NGN 95,000 complete Kit for exactly one eligible role. The Kit must be useful with zero marketplace candidates. No sign-up/payment required just to see deliverables, price, and limitations. No recurring marketplace promotion for a saved role until ≥5 real, opted-in matching candidates exist and the eligibility check passes server-side. That promotion threshold is deliberately **not** the same number as the checkout gate, which the founder lowered to one on 2026-09-02 with a disclosure attached — what may be advertised and what an informed buyer may choose to buy are different questions, and the two numbers should not be reconciled by changing either. No **fifth** role track, consumer plan, annual plan, free trial, discount, coupon, commission marketplace, new service, or custom enterprise project at launch. This clause said "no fourth role track" until 2026-09-04; `mlops` became the fourth on 2026-08-25 by founder amendment and has shipped since Phase 7, so the sentence was barring something that already existed. "At the original prices" is likewise gone: prices have been raised three times by founder decision, and the current set is fixed in CLAUDE.md.

## 3. Low-user revenue discipline

Scorecard: paying employer organizations, net collected revenue, gross contribution, retained MRR, refund/dispute rate, useful paid outcomes. Candidate registrations alone are not revenue proof. Per paid SKU, record price, Paystack fee, refund/dispute allowance, marginal AWS/AI cost, support time, net contribution — without exposing financial secrets. A paid flow passes only if it delivers the promised artifact/entitlement, stays recoverable, and shows positive expected contribution at approved cost assumptions — not merely that checkout works.

## 4. Demand and competition gates

[[docs/COMMERCIAL_SCORECARD]] tracks, one row per version, dated: distinct employer diagnostics; complete previews; Kit checkout starts; verified Kit payments; successful Kit deliveries/downloads; usefulness responses; refunds/disputes; share-created visits; share-created diagnostics; eligible subscription roles; paid subscriptions; renewal/churn; total AWS cost per paying employer.

"Initial paid demand validated" (internal only) requires ≥10 verified Kit purchases from ≥5 unrelated employer organizations within a recorded 60-day window, ≥6 purchasers marking the Kit useful, and no unresolved critical delivery/refund/dispute/privacy/security pattern. This is an operational gate, not scientific proof, and never a public marketing claim.

"Self-marketing loop evidenced" (internal only) requires ≥3 new employer diagnostics honestly attributed to consented product sharing, with no paid ads, spam, purchased lists, or fabricated attribution.

Competition is never described as "low" without a dated competitor/substitute review plus ≥5 unrelated employer conversations showing existing alternatives don't adequately solve the proof-first hiring job. Record alternatives and objections; never attack competitors or copy protected material.

If a gate fails: keep the niche and core offer stable long enough to learn; improve the smallest evidence-backed friction; do not respond with more features, roles, providers, or ad spend.

## 5. Product-led self-marketing loop

**Employer loop**: free role diagnostic → useful proof-gap snapshot → optional privacy-safe share → recipient can create a separate diagnostic → paid Kit → optional privacy-safe Kit summary share → marketplace offer only after the five-match gate.

**Candidate loop**: free proof profile → explicit public-share consent → privacy-safe proof URL → optional verifier invitation → recipient can create their own free candidate profile or employer diagnostic. Sharing is revocable and rate-limited.

Every shared surface strips private role notes, candidate contact details, non-consented evidence, secrets, and tracking parameters; includes accurate social metadata, a clear Zoryvex source label, and one honest next action. First-party, privacy-minimized attribution only — minimum pseudonymous source token, with retention/consent controls, no third-party analytics/fingerprinting/cross-site tracking. No paid referrals at launch. Ranking, founding status, access, entitlement, or purchased value never depends on sharing. No dark patterns, forced invitations, contact scraping, mass messages, or fake scarcity.

## 6. No-waste cost rules

Zero paid advertising at launch. No new account, service, MCP, analytics tool, CRM, email-marketing tool, referral platform, or hosted product. Before any billable resource or optional feature: one best recommendation with user value, current official price basis, minimum/expected/worst-case monthly cost, free-tier dependency, shutdown method, data effect, rollback — founder approval required where the active prompt requires it.

An AWS budget/alarm only warns, it never caps spend by itself, unless an independently tested control actually disables the permitted workload; preserve security, backups, logs, and evidence needed for safe operation regardless.

Keep test resources temporary and tagged; stop/remove only what the original guide authorizes, only after evidence is saved; never destroy state, logs, backups, or production data merely to cut cost.

Bedrock stays off when deterministic output satisfies the promise; enable a model only when a versioned evaluation proves material usefulness improvement within a recorded per-paid-output cap, and disable automatically on quality/cost threshold failure.

No scaling infrastructure, adding a region, reserving capacity, or adding always-on compute based on projected users — scale only from measured traffic/latency/errors/quotas/cost evidence through the original approval workflow.

At least monthly: identify unused/unprofitable features and propose the single safest reversible removal or simplification. Never keep an unused feature to avoid admitting sunk cost.

## 7. Beginner implementation

One best next action in plain English, no menu unless the decision is legally/financially irreversible — recommend one option first, explain the irreversible consequence in one sentence if a choice is required. Every safe code/test/doc/Git/IaC action performed by the assistant, never handed to the founder. Unavoidable founder actions keep the single `HUMAN ACTION REQUIRED` format — exact breadcrumbs, exact non-secret values, expected result, reply "done," never combined. Maintain [[docs/COMMERCIAL_SCORECARD]], [[docs/COST_GUARDRAILS]], [[PRODUCT_SPEC]], `ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md`, threat model, data model, copy, and tests as affected. Every commercial metric explained in baby-simple language inside this runbook.

## 8. Acceptance and stop conditions

No phase passes if this addendum's requirements for that phase are missing, contradicted, or untested — applied at the smallest relevant phase, never reopening unrelated passed work. Phase mapping: **Prompt 0** records this addendum and the permanent rules (this document). **Prompt 3** specifies the commercial scorecard/measurement model. **Prompt 4** implements the primary offer, truthful copy, and share surfaces locally. **Prompt 5** proves current official cost assumptions and kill switches. **Prompt 7** implements secure share/attribution and authorization. **Prompt 8** preserves fixed Paystack prices and proves positive expected contribution. **Prompt 10** audits truth, demand evidence, unit economics, privacy, legal, and cost gates. **Prompt 11** launches the Kit funnel before eligible subscriptions. **Prompt 12** measures, learns, and optimizes without ads or new services.

A high user count, a working homepage, a successful payment, or a passing build is never by itself proof of commercial success — record facts, uncertainty, and counter-evidence. If revenue is zero, unit contribution is non-positive, repeated usefulness is weak, refund/dispute patterns appear, or sharing causes privacy/abuse problems: stop growth changes, preserve service already owed, safely disable the affected acquisition/paid feature, record the evidence, and recommend the single smallest reversible repair.

Never weaken the preserved legal/compliance gate to make money — if commercial GO is not evidenced, preserve SAFE-SHELL exactly. Never publicly claim Zoryvex is the best, high-demand, low-competition, self-marketing, profitable, proven, or guaranteed unless the precise claim is current, measured, reviewable, and allowed by the original guide.
