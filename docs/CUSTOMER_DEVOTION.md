# Customer Devotion Objective

Optimize for users who would be genuinely disappointed to lose the specific proof and hiring value Zoryvex gives them — never for addiction, engagement, or time-on-site. If a metric goes up because someone is stuck, confused, or anxious, it is the wrong metric.

## First value, by side of the market

- **Employer, first value**: a useful proof-gap snapshot from a short role diagnostic — free, no candidate profiles required.
- **Candidate, first value**: one structured proof item, plus a private proof preview and an explicit consent choice about sharing it.

## Output principles (every artifact Zoryvex produces must be)

- **Versioned** — a user can see what changed and when.
- **Explainable** — the reasoning behind a score/match is inspectable, not a black box.
- **Shareable only with consent** — nothing about a candidate leaves their control by default.
- **Revocable** — consent can be withdrawn and it actually stops sharing, going forward.
- **Accessible** — usable with assistive technology; this is a correctness requirement, not a nice-to-have.
- **Honest about uncertainty** — no false precision; show confidence, not fake certainty.

## What to track (devotion signals)

- Time to first useful artifact (employer diagnostic result, candidate proof item).
- Repeat useful actions (not repeat visits — repeat actions that produced value).
- Voluntary, privacy-safe sharing (a candidate choosing to share, unprompted by dark patterns).
- Kit delivery rate and Kit usefulness (did the employer act on it).
- Marketplace eligibility reached (five real, current, discovery-opted matching profiles for a saved role — see [[PRODUCT_SPEC]]).
- Renewal and churn, refunds and disputes.
- AWS cost per paying employer (ties to [[COST_GUARDRAILS]]).

## What NOT to optimize

- Page views, session length, streaks, notification-driven return visits, or any form of pressure/urgency to act.
- Raw engagement as a proxy for value — it is not the same thing and will be actively resisted as a north star.

## Standing constraint on every checkout / value surface

Every paid moment must show, before any charge: concrete deliverables, exact limits, final price, seller disclosure (Paystack processes under **Abba's Solutions**), cancellation/refund behavior, and one clear next action. Never a fake testimonial, counter, urgency, guarantee, hidden fee, forced trial, or obstructed cancellation. This applies to the Kit, the Founding tiers, and voluntary support alike.
