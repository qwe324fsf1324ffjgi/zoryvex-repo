# VIDEO 08 — "What an employer actually sees"

**The transparency video**, aimed at candidates. Removes the main hesitation
about joining: *who sees my details?*

- **Length:** 30s · **Format:** screen recording, employer side · **One idea:** they see the proof, not the person
- **Claimable:** yes — this is literally the screen

## Hook — second zero

An employer's shortlist on screen. Every row reads `Candidate a1b2c3`.

Caption: **"This is what an employer sees. No name."**

## Shot list

| Time | Vision | Caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:04 | Shortlist of anonymous ids | "This is what an employer sees. No name." |
| 0:04–0:09 | Open a row: the claims, and what was checked on each | "They see what we checked." |

| 0:28–0:30 | Static | "You're not a row in a database someone bought." |

## The line that does the work

> "You're not a row in a database someone bought."

It names the thing people actually fear about job platforms, in their own
words, and the screen recording has already proved it before the sentence
lands.

## Claim discipline

Everything here must match the shipped screens exactly. If the UI changes,
this video becomes a false claim, so **re-shoot it rather than re-use it.**

Do not imply the employer can never see a name. They can, after the candidate
answers, and saying so plainly is what makes the rest believable.
