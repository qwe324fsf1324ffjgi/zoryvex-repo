## Why

The number-editing is universally understood and slightly uncomfortable —
almost everyone has considered rounding up. The discomfort holds attention
without manipulation.

The pivot at 0:11 is the whole video. Before it: a document nobody checks.
After it: a thing that cannot be typed.

## Do not

Do not imply most people lie. There is no evidence for that and the claim is
both unkind and unsupported. The point is not that people lie. The point is
that **nothing verifies it either way**, which is a statement about the
document, not about people.
