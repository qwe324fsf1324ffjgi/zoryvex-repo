## Claim discipline — read this

- **Attribute on screen**, every time, not only in the voice-over
- **Do not** extend it to Nigeria, to any named company, or to any number
- **Do not** say "this is happening to you"
- **Do not** use flags, hoodie-hacker stock, or news-alert graphics. Plain
  text on the brand ground. The restraint is what makes it credible
- **Do not** imply Zoryvex prevents this. Zoryvex verifies specific claims
  against specific sources. It is not an identity-fraud product, and claiming
  otherwise would be exactly the kind of overreach the brand forbids

## Why the restraint is strategic, not timid

Fear content gets reach and loses trust. Stating a documented finding flatly,
with attribution, and then declining to oversell the implication, is more
persuasive to the person who actually buys software than an alarming edit
would be.
