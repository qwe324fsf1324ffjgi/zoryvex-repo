# VIDEO 01 — "A claim and a source are different things"

**The foundational one.** If only one video is ever made, make this one.

- **Length:** 24s
- **Format:** vertical, screen recording + hands on keyboard
- **Platforms:** TikTok, Reels, Shorts, LinkedIn
- **One idea:** a CV is a claim; a source is a source
- **Claimable:** yes, describes only what the product does

---

## The hook — frame one, second zero

No greeting. No logo. No face. The first frame is a text field with a cursor
in it, and a hand typing:

> `AWS Certified Solutions Architect`

Caption, burned in, top third: **"Watch me lie on a CV."**

The typing is the hook. It is happening before the viewer has decided to
watch, which is the point.

---

## Shot list

| Time | Vision | Audio / caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:03 | Hands typing the certification name into a plain field | "Watch me lie on a CV." |
| 0:03–0:06 | Field filled. Cursor blinking. Nothing happens | "Done. That's a CV." |
| 0:06–0:09 | Cut to the same claim in Zoryvex. A **Check this** control | "Now watch it get checked." |
| 0:09–0:14 | Click. Brief pending state. Result returns: **not found** | "It asks the body that issued it." |
| 0:14–0:18 | The claim sits there, unverified, clearly labelled | "I can still say it. It just doesn't count." |
| 0:18–0:22 | Cut to a real verified claim. Green mark appears **once** | "This one was checked." |
| 0:22–0:24 | Static end frame: the two claims side by side, one marked | "Claim. Source. Different things." |

---

## Why it is built this way

- **The lie is the hook.** Not a question, not a statistic, not a promise.
  Something slightly transgressive happening on screen in second zero.
- **The failure is shown before the success.** Most product videos show the
  happy path. Showing the check *failing first* is what makes the success
  believable four seconds later.
- **Green appears once**, at 0:18. Per the brand system, green means verified.
  If it appeared earlier it would be decoration.
- **The best beat is at 0:18 of 24** — about 75% through, then a quiet close.
  Do not put it at the end.

## Captions

Burned in, system stack, high weight, upper third so a platform's own UI does
not cover them. Assume the video is watched silent.

## Do not

- Do not say "seamless", "unlock", or "powered by AI"
- Do not add a countdown, a "wait for it", or a swipe-up prompt
- Do not name a real person's certificate, real or invented
- Do not claim any customer, any outcome, or any number
